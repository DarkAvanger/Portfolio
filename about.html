About Me

Junior Rigger & Technical Artist

I am a 24-year-old Junior Rigger and Technical Artist based in Terrassa, Spain. With a Master’s degree in Rigging and Character FX and a Bachelor’s in Video Game Design and Development, my focus is solving the complex technical puzzles that allow animators and artists to work without friction.

I thrive in environments that require both creative problem-solving and strict technical optimization. I recently served as the Rigging Supervisor for the Steam release of Not My War, ensuring seamless asset compatibility and pipeline efficiency.

My background also includes detail-oriented roles like Localization Editing for AAA titles like Black Myth: Wukong. This gave me a holistic understanding of the game development pipeline—from early technical setup to the final shipped product.

The Toolset

  • Software: Autodesk Maya, Unreal Engine 5, Unity
  • Languages: Python (Tool automation, custom UIs)
  • Specialties: Creature & Mechanical Rigging, Proxy Workflows
  • Other: Engine Integration, Pipeline Management
